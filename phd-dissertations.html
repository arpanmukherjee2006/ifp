Ph.D. Program

The Institute of Forest Productivity offers doctoral research opportunities in various aspects of forestry science, productivity enhancement, and sustainable forest management.

Our Ph.D. program focuses on cutting-edge research in forest ecology, agroforestry, climate change adaptation, and innovative forestry technologies.

Research Areas

  • • Forest Productivity Enhancement
  • • Agroforestry Systems
  • • Climate Change and Forest Adaptation
  • • Sustainable Forest Management
  • • Forest Ecology and Biodiversity
