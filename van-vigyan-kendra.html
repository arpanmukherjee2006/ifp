Van Vigyan Kendra

A network of field centres delivering forestry training and quality planting materials to support sustainable livelihoods and ecological restoration across Jharkhand, Bihar and West Bengal.

About VVK

Established by ICFRE under CAMPA (MoEF&CC), VVKs aim to bridge the gap between forestry research and field implementation. They translate scientific research into practical, field-ready technologies for ecological restoration, afforestation, and livelihood enhancement.

These centers also focus on capacity building, demonstration of improved practices, and promoting sustainable forest-based income opportunities for rural communities.

Key Stakeholders

The major stakeholders include farmers, tribal communities, forest departments, research institutes, and rural youth entrepreneurs. These groups actively participate in training, demonstrations, and implementation of forestry-based livelihood activities.

Their collaboration ensures better knowledge transfer, community participation, and long-term sustainability of forest and agroforestry initiatives.
