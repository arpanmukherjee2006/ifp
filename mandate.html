Mandate

Vision

To develop and disseminates technological interventions for augmented eco-restoration, fragile forest ecosystem conservation, forest productivity enhancement, climate resilient agroforestry, rural life quality improvement and ethno-ecological applications.

Mission

Undertake research on eco-restoration and rehabilitation, genetic improvement & resources mapping of important forest tree species, NTFPs, invasive species and LKTs, silviculture of important tree species, forest health & capacity building extension programme.
