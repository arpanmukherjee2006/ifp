Genetics and Tree Improvement

Advancing forest productivity through cutting-edge genetic research and innovative tree improvement programs

Focus

Genetic improvement of important agroforestry and forestry tree species

Exploration, evaluation and conservation of forest genetic resources

Standardization of micro propagation protocols for mass multiplication of RET and commercially important tree species

Population genetics studies and molecular interventions in tree improvement programmes & conservation of genetic resources

Vision

Develop productive and climate-resilient clones/varieties, conserve forest genetic resources and establish agroforestry models to improve profitability of the farm
