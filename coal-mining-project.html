Project Title

Standardization of Package and Practices for Eco-rehabilitation of Degraded Coal Mines Falling Under Different Agro-climatic Zones of India through Forestry Interventions

Principal Investigator

Dr. Anshuman Das, Scientist C

Funding Agency

Coal India Ltd., Kolkata

Project Duration

5 Years (2024 – 2029)

Project Description

Eco-rehabilitation of degraded coal mine lands through forestry interventions across different agro-climatic zones of Jharkhand, Odisha, and West Bengal.
