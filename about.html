About the Institute

The Institute of Forest Productivity (IFP)

The Institute of Forest Productivity (IFP) is a premier research institute situated in Ranchi in the state of Jharkhand. It operates under the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education (ICFRE) of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India.

Established as a center of excellence in forest research, IFP is dedicated to advancing scientific knowledge and developing innovative solutions for sustainable forest management, conservation, and productivity enhancement. The institute plays a crucial role in addressing contemporary challenges in forestry through cutting-edge research and technology development.

Our multidisciplinary approach encompasses forest ecology, genetics, biotechnology, and sustainable management practices, contributing significantly to India's forest conservation efforts and environmental sustainability goals.

Research & Development

IFP Ranchi - Historical Journey

Tracing our path of excellence in forest research and sustainable development

1988

Institute Establishment

Institute of Forest Productivity established in Ranchi as a premier research institution under ICFRE to advance forest science and sustainable management practices.

1995

Research Programs Launch

Launched comprehensive research programs in forest genetics, tree improvement, and silviculture, establishing IFP as a center of excellence in forestry research.

2005

Technology Integration

Integrated advanced biotechnology and molecular techniques in forest research, pioneering innovative approaches to tree breeding and conservation.

2015

Global Partnerships

Established international collaborations and partnerships, expanding research scope and contributing to global forest conservation initiatives.

2020

Digital Innovation

Embraced digital transformation with AI-driven research methodologies, remote sensing technologies, and data-driven forest management solutions.

Present

Sustainable Future

Leading India's forest research with cutting-edge science, sustainable practices, and innovative solutions for climate change mitigation and biodiversity conservation.
