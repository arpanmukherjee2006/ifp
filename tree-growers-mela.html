Tree Growers Mela

Events designed to connect growers, practitioners, and experts around forestry-based livelihoods and practices.

Tree Growers Mela

Organized under the supervision of the ICFRE- Institute of Forest Productivity, Ranchi.

Key Focus Areas

  • • Sessions on climate resilience and soil conservation
  • • Trainings and Demonstrations emphasizing nursery management, agroforestry, organic pest control and biodiversity-friendly practices
  • • Focus on medicinal & aromatic plants alongside timber and multi-use species
  • • Promotion of agroforestry and indigenous species

Participation & Engagement

  • • Participation: tribal farmers, women SHGs, forest officials
  • • Youth and women engagement for income-generating activities
  • • Stakeholder meetings and collaborative programs
  • • Bamboo producer engagement and networking
